Wife

Full-time Permanent Family & Happiness 1 position

We are currently looking for a highly motivated individual to join our organization in a long-term strategic role as Wife. The successful candidate will work closely with the CTO and will play a critical role in the organization's long-term happiness, emotional stability, and daily operations.

This is a permanent position with significant opportunities for personal growth, affection, travel, movie nights, cuddles, and spontaneous adventures. No experience required — comprehensive training provided directly by the husband.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain a strong relationship with the CTO.
  • Provide emotional support during difficult situations.
  • Participate in regular movie nights.
  • Collaborate on future travel plans.
  • Maintain excellent communication at all times.
  • Tolerate occasional nonsense from management.
  • Participate in spontaneous adventures, on short notice.
  • Provide hugs when operationally required.
  • Ensure the CTO receives adequate affection on a daily basis.
  • Participate in long-term strategic planning, including building a life together.
